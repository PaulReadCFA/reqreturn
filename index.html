    Required return and dividend projection over time

    This chart displays the projected dividend cash flows over 10 years with the calculated required return. The dividend stream continues until infinity, but only the first 10 years are shown. At year 0, the initial stock purchase price is shown as a negative cash flow (money paid out). At each subsequent year, dividend payments grow at the specified growth rate. The dashed purple line shows the constant required return percentage on the right axis.
