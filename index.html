    Required return and dividend projection over time

    This chart displays the projected dividend cash flows over 10 years with the calculated required return. The dividend stream continues indefinitely, but only the first 10 years are shown. At year 0, the initial stock purchase price is shown as a negative cash flow (money paid out). At each subsequent year, dividend payments grow at the specified growth rate. The dashed purple line shows the constant required return percentage on the right axis.

    Keyboard navigation: Use Left and Right arrow keys to move between years. Press Home to jump to year 0, or End to jump to the final year. Each year will announce its dividend amount, required return, and total cash flow.
